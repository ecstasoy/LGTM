# NNNN. State the decision as the title

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-short-title.md) | Deprecated
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD (when the decision was made)

## Context

The problem and the forces at play: constraints, costs, and what was known at the time. Use the terms defined in `CONTEXT.md`.

## Decision

What we do, in full sentences.

## Alternatives considered

What was rejected or deferred, and why. Write "None recorded" rather than inventing alternatives after the fact.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and the costs we accept, including known gaps.

## Sources

Pull requests, commits and files that show the decision.
